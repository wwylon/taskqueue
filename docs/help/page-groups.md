The **Groups** tab aggregates tasks by YAML `group` field. Stop or retry all members of a group from here, or jump to the queue with a group filter applied (**View in queue** also selects the group's project).

Groups are labels for operations — they do not change scheduler priority. Compare with **suite** for enable/disable of related tasks.
